### **06\. Customer Personas & Tailored Recommendations**

*Breaking down the target market into actionable segments, highlighting their specific pain points, enablers, and the tailored recommendations for each.*

#### **Persona 1: First Jobbers (The Pragmatic Early Adopter)**

* **Profile:**   
  * **Demographics:** Male, 24–30 years old, 20K–40K THB/month income. Lives in urban areas, often condominiums or apartments. First-time buyer or replacing a family car. Financially independent.  
  * **Psychographics:** Lead fast-paced urban lifestyles balancing work and social life. Highly interested in technology and innovation.  
  * **Behavioral:** Highly research-driven, comparing specifications, ownership costs, reviews, and competitor offerings before making a decision. Regular consumers of YouTube reviews, TikTok content, automotive influencers, and online EV communities. Considers promotions, freebies, and ownership benefits when comparing offers, but does not make decisions based solely on giveaways. Brand-agnostic (open to all brands)  
* **Insights (what they want):**  
  * A reliable and affordable vehicle for daily urban commute (10–30 km).  
  * Trustworthy after-sales service without months-long wait times.  
  * Low running costs (already calculates electricity vs. fuel savings).  
  * Neutral interior color options (prefers minimal over colorful colors).  
* **Barriers:**  
  * After-sales quality concerns  
  * Unclear warranty coverage and hidden ownership costs (warranty opacity)  
  * Vehicle designs or interiors that appear to be too flashy or unconventional  
* **Recommendations:**   
  * **Campaign Names:** Don’t Guess. Calculate.  
  * **Taglines/Messages:** Smart Decisions Start with Real Numbers, Make your first EV decision with confidence   
  * **Supporting Message:** Transparent Total Cost of Ownership   
  * **Main Idea:** EV Cost Calculator (website)  
    * Input: interested product/car model, salary, distance driven per day, previous car’s (ICE) fuel expenses, home charging?  
    * Output: monthly savings  
  * **Channels:** Dealership QR codes, online EV communities, Google Search  
  * **Product-Fit:** Atto 1, Atto 2, Atto 3, Dolphin  
  * **Purpose:** help users realize that they can actually afford an EV, and it may cost less than their current transportation  
  * **Main Question:** Can I afford this?

#### **Persona 2: Family with No Kids (The Couple)**

* **Profile:**   
  * **Demographics:** Male and female (couple), 30–40 years old, 60K–100K THB/month combined income. Share major financial decisions. Most own one vehicle and are considering upgrading to a larger, more versatile vehicle. Weekday commuting and weekend travel. House dwellers. Joint purchase decision makers. Lifestyle includes provincial travel and visiting family.  
  * **Psychographics:** Value comfort, convenience, flexibility, and quality time. Visiting family in other provinces. Financially responsible but willing to invest in products that offer long-term value and reduce stress.  
  * **Behavioral:** Prioritize convenience and reliability over cutting-edge technology alone. Research vehicles through YouTube reviews, facebook groups, and recommendations from family and friends.  
* **Insights (what they want):**  
  * A vehicle that supports both city commuting and weekend adventures  
  * Stress-free (peace of mind) weekend travel without charging limitations.  
  * Flexibility without changing their driving habits   
    * BEV efficiency for city weekdays  
    *  Fuel backup for long trips (PHEV is the ideal solution).  
  * Mutual comfort and agreement (joint decision).  
* **Barriers:**  
  * Charging infrastructure outside major cities (charging anxiety on provincial trips) \>\>\> CONVENIENCE  
  * Lack of understanding on how PHEVs work  
  * Risk of a "joint veto" if one partner has a major concern

  * **Taglines/Messages**: Built for weekdays. Ready for weekends, From everyday moments to unforgettable weekends, Drive electric Monday to Friday. Drive anywhere Saturday and Sunday

    * Combine relatable lifestyle experiences with technical explanations  
    * BYD has already done the campaign “One Tank, One Charge” to prove the car's capability. But for the family with no kids segment, the challenge is not about range but more about lifestyle  
      * Instead of “how far can it go?”, focus on “how does it fit into their lives”  
      * Trackings of electricity used, fuel consumption, cost per km, charging frequency  
      * Weekdays: commute to work, grocery shopping, gym, charging behavior  
      * Weekends: visiting parents, travelling to other provinces  
  * **Channels:** Car YouTubers and Facebook Travel Content

#### **Persona 3: Family with Kids**

* **Profile:**  
  * **Demographics:** Families aged 32-45 years old with school-aged children, household income of 80K–150K. Typically own Japanese MPVs or SUVs (e.g., Fortuner, Carnival, Xpander). Most live in suburban areas or provincial cities.   
  * **Psychographics:** Prioritize family comfort, reliability, safety, and practicality. Value peace of mind. Regularly travel with children for school activities, family visits, and weekend trips. Risk-averse   
  * **Behavioral:** Purchasing decisions are shared between both parents, with children's needs heavily influencing vehicle selection.  
* **Insights (what they want):**  
  * A car that makes family trips easier (peace of mind)  
  * A car that can complete long distance travels without stopping to charge, so sleeping children are not disturbed.  
  * Expansive boot capacity for strollers, school bags, weekly shopping, and weekend luggages (space and utility)

  * Current market perception (noted in research) that current Thai BYD models are "too small" for larger family requirements; lack of a clear 7-seat option or sufficient boot space is a hard blocker.
